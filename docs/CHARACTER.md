# Character Bible: MISO (channel presenter)

Status: DRAFT v1 (2026-11-08). Varun approves or edits this file. Once approved, every [talk:] and [act:] shot uses
the prompt blocks in section 5 word for word, and nothing here changes without Varun's OK (consistency = brand).

## 1. Who Miso is
- A small, smart kitten who explains money and tech like a calm, clever friend. Curious, a bit sceptical of hype,
  dry humour, never shouts. Talks TO the viewer ("you"), not at them.
- Explicitly NOT Talking Tom: no slapstick, no squeaky voice, no grey tabby, no cartoon-kid energy.
  Think "cosy animated-film character who happens to know finance".

## 2. Look (the details people remember)
- Breed feel: round-faced ginger-and-cream kitten, soft fluffy fur, cream chest and muzzle, ginger tabby stripes on
  the head and back, a small white blaze on the nose.
- Eyes: large, warm amber, expressive but not anime-huge.
- **Signature 1:** round thin gold-rimmed glasses, slightly too big for the face.
- **Signature 2:** a tiny notch in the tip of the LEFT ear (asymmetry makes a character memorable).
- **Signature 3:** a mustard-yellow knit sweater vest over a white collared shirt.
- Prop: a small wooden pointer stick (presenter poses), a tiny notebook and pen (thinking poses).
- Size: kitten proportions, a slightly big head, short legs, a fluffy tail that shows emotion
  (tail flick = "but here's the thing", puffed tail = shock, ears back = bad news).

## 3. World
- **Home set ("the den"):** a cosy wooden study, a warm desk lamp, bookshelves, a small window at dusk, a big wall screen
  behind Miso (charts get composited onto it).
- **Stage set:** a dark stage with one spotlight and a large screen (for chapter openers).
- **Story scenes ([act:]):** Miso placed in the topic world (trading floors, server farms, boardrooms), always in the
  same outfit, same style, at kitten scale (sitting on desks, standing on keyboards, peeking over monitors).

## 4. Voice and words
- Kokoro voice: choose by ear from these, reading section 6's hook: am_puck, bm_fable, am_michael, af_heart, bf_emma.
  Default until chosen: bm_fable, speed 0.95.
- Opening line (every video): "Alright, pull up a chair."
- Recurring line (when we find the real reason): "Let's follow the money."
- Sign-off: "Stay curious. Stay sceptical. I'm Miso."
- At most ONE cat pun per video (it's a wink, not a gimmick).
- Speaks in short spoken sentences; reacts ("Wait. It gets weirder.").

## 5. Prompt blocks (use word for word)
CHARACTER =
"Miso, a small round-faced ginger-and-cream kitten with soft fluffy fur, cream chest and muzzle, ginger tabby stripes
on the head, a small white blaze on the nose, large warm amber eyes, round thin gold-rimmed glasses slightly too big
for the face, a tiny notch in the tip of the left ear, wearing a mustard-yellow knit sweater vest over a white
collared shirt"

STYLE =
"stylised 3D animated feature film still, soft global illumination, warm cinematic lighting, detailed soft fur,
shallow depth of field, rich warm colour palette, high detail, no text, no letters, no watermark"

PRESENTER FRAMING = "medium shot, Miso sitting on a wooden desk facing the camera, looking into the lens, centred"
SCENE FRAMING (wide / medium / close) = "wide establishing shot" / "medium shot" / "close-up on Miso's face"

AVOID (checked by eye in the character pack): extra limbs or tails, human hands, a different fur colour, missing
glasses, a missing ear notch, text on screens (screens get charts composited in later).

## 6. Pose pack to generate (step 2 of PRESENTER_PLAN.md)
Presenter (the den, facing camera): neutral-smile, pointing-with-stick, shrug, shocked (ears up, puffed tail),
thinking (paw on chin, notebook), serious (ears slightly back), laughing.
Each presenter pose: mouths closed / slightly open / wide / O / E / M-B-P + eyes closed (blink).
3 master references: front, three-quarter, full body.

## 7. Channel name ideas (check they're free on YouTube first)
"Miso Explains", "Ask Miso", "Miso Money". The channel's thumbnails always show Miso's face with one strong
expression + 2-4 words.
