- number | 5H | label                   a giant magenta number counting up + a label. Every key number.
- tunnel | WORD                         frames flying at the camera around one word. Speed, depth, "and then".
- words | the sentence with *one* key word   word by word on the spoken words, *word* in a lime box.
- endcard | WORD | small line           the closing lockup + a "COMMENT <WORD>" pill. Only the last scene.
Use only these kinds: anything else is a Check error.

WRITING RULES
- Length: Shorts must be 15 s-2:00; aim for 30-50 s (Check warns under 30 s). At speed 1.15 the voice says ~180 words a minute:
  30 s = ~90 words, 45 s = ~135.
- One sentence per scene, 4-14 words. A scene over ~6 seconds (about 18 words) is a Check warning: split it.
- The words in the tag should be words the narration actually says in that sentence (they land when spoken).
  Keep tag text short: headlines 1-3 words a line, stack/nodes items 1-2 words each.
- Vary the kinds: never the same kind 3 scenes in a row. Start with headline, end with endcard, and put a
  number on every key number.
- The last line asks the viewer to comment the endcard WORD ("Want the prompt? Comment motion.").
- Facts only from your research list. No real people's quotes you can't source, no logos.
- Write for the ear: contractions, numbers as words in the narration, no stage directions, no emojis.
