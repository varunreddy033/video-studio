## SCENE 1 | STREET | NIGHT
[cast: KAI at left, VEX at right]
NARRATOR: Harbour Bay. Three years after the storm, the street lights still hum a little too loud.
NARRATOR: Most people stopped hearing it. Kai never did.
KAI (nervous): Okay. Okay okay okay. You can stop buzzing now.
KAI (worried, close): Something is wrong with you tonight. You sound scared.
VEX (calm): They usually are, this time of night.
KAI (shocked): Whoa. Where did you come from?
VEX (smug): The same place as you. The dark part of the street.
KAI (nervous): Right. Cool. Totally normal thing to say.
VEX (calm): You can hear them, can't you? The lights.
KAI (confused): I don't know what you mean.
VEX (cold, close): Your hand is shaking, and the lamp above you is shaking with it.
KAI (determined): Who are you?
VEX (menacing): Call me Vex. I used to work at the power station. Before the storm.
VEX (calm): I'm not here to hurt anyone. I'm here to ask you one question.
KAI (worried): That is exactly what someone says before they hurt someone.
VEX (smirk): Fair. Here is the question. What do the wires say to you?
KAI (quiet): They say they're tired. Every night they say they're tired.
VEX (cold): Then let them rest. Everything that hums can be switched off.

## SCENE 2 | KAIS_ROOM | NIGHT
[cast: KAI at centre]
NARRATOR: Kai ran home. He did not look back once, which he later admitted was a mistake.
KAI (scared): Lock the door. Lock the window. Unplug everything.
KAI (worried): Okay. Desk lamp. Please tell me you're not tired too.
NARRATOR: The desk lamp flickered twice. Then it did something it had never done before.
KAI (shocked, close): Did you just say my name?
KAI (whispering): Okay. If you can hear me, blink once for yes.
NARRATOR: It blinked once.
KAI (determined): That man, Vex. He wants to switch you off. All of you. The whole city.
KAI (sad): And I don't even know how to stop a vacuum cleaner.
KAI (hopeful): But you know things. You were there in the storm, weren't you?
NARRATOR: The lamp went dark for a whole second. In this house, that counted as a very long yes.
KAI (determined): Then you can show me where it started.
NARRATOR: The lamp blinked three times. Kai didn't know what three meant yet. He would soon.

## SCENE 3 | SQUARE | DAWN
[cast: KAI at left, VEX at right]
NARRATOR: Morning. The old clock in the square had not moved since the storm. Until today.
KAI (shocked): The clock is ticking. Nobody has fixed it in three years.
VEX (calm): Nobody fixed it. Something woke it up. Something that talks to you.
KAI (determined): You followed me.
VEX (smug): I didn't have to. The whole city goes quiet when you are scared, Kai.
KAI (shocked): How do you know my name?
VEX (calm): The same way the lamp does. You say it to yourself when you are nervous.
KAI (nervous): That is not a normal answer.
KAI (angry): Stay away from the lights. Stay away from me.
VEX (cold, close): That depends on you. Tonight at midnight, the station switches off.
VEX (calm): Come and watch, or come and stop me. Either way, come alone.
KAI (determined, close): I'll be there. And I won't be alone.
NARRATOR: Above them, every street light in Harbour Bay flickered at the same time.
NARRATOR: Kai did not know it yet, but the city had just picked a side.
