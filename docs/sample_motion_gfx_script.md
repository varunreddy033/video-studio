## The claims

[ai: a newspaper report lying on a desk]

[ai: crates of computer chips at a border crossing at night]
The claim is that she was used to get Nvidia chips into China, against US export rules.
[ai: two people talking quietly at a dinner table]
The Financial Times reported a memo saying the two had a romantic relationship.
[card: "HONEY TRAP" | what some outlets now call it]
Some outlets now call it a honey trap. That is their word, not a finding.
