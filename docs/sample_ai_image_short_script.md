[ai: a red octopus spreading its arms over dark rocks on the sea floor, soft blue light from above, close-up]
An octopus has three hearts, and its blood is blue.
[number: 3 | hearts in one octopus]
Two small hearts push blood through the gills.
[ai: the delicate feathery gills of an octopus under its mantle, macro shot, dark water, gentle light]
The third one sends it to the rest of the body.
[card: BLUE BLOOD | copper, not iron]
The blood is blue because it carries oxygen with copper instead of iron.
[ai: an octopus jetting away through open blue water, arms trailing behind, a cloud of ink, wide shot]
And here is the strange part. When it swims, the main heart stops beating.
[card: SWIMMING TIRES IT OUT | so it would rather crawl]
That is why an octopus would rather crawl than swim.
[ai: an octopus crawling slowly across white sand between shells, low angle, morning light through the water]
So next time you see one crawling, remember: an octopus has three hearts.
